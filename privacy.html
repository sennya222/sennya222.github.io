Privacy policy - Pochta Sekretar

Last updated: 11 November 2026.

  • The app requests read-only access (gmail.readonly) to mailboxes that belong to its owner. It never sends, deletes, moves or labels messages.
  • Message data is used only to sort the owner's own mail into the owner's work folders. It is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, and not used for advertising or for training AI models.
  • Access tokens are stored on the owner's private server. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
